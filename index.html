Project 1: Retro Raster Run

Rehman Khalid, Jace Schubert | Pinhole camera, line rasterization, triangle filling, and z-buffering using Canvas 2D only.

←→ move left/right   ↑↓ move forward/back   Space collect nearby crystal   R reset   1 high-res wireframe   2 320×200 raster lines   3 filled triangles

Goal: move through the scene and collect the glowing crystal objects. The camera translates in X and Z only and never rotates.
